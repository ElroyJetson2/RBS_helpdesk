How to log an incident

Simple guide for residents and property owners

You can report a maintenance issue online at any time. You will receive a unique reference number so you can follow progress until the job is closed. Photos of the problem are welcome and help our team respond faster.

Step by step

  1. Open the portal — go to the Report page and start a new request.
  2. Enter the property address — start typing and choose a verified address from the suggestions.
  3. Your name, phone and email — both a 10-digit phone number and a valid email are required.
  4. Describe the issue — say what is wrong, where it is, and if it is urgent.
  5. Add photos (optional) — up to 5 photos. Tap the × on a photo if you selected the wrong one.
  6. Submit — press Submit Incident. Copy and keep your reference number.

Track your request

  1. Open Track and enter your reference number.
  2. You will see status (Open or Closed), staff notes, and any photos.
  3. Tap a photo to view a larger version.

Tips

  • One request per issue so each job can be tracked clearly.
  • For urgent matters (water, power, safety), say so in the description.
  • Keep your reference number until the job shows as Closed.
